WarmPath — Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026

WarmPath is a personal job-search tool built and run by Soren Larsen. It runs locally on his own computer. It has one user, and that user is its author. It is not a product, it is not offered to anyone else, and it has no sign-up.

What it accesses

WarmPath asks for two read-only Google permissions, for the author's own Google Account only:

  • gmail.readonly — to read job-alert emails and application confirmation and rejection emails in the author's own mailbox, so that job postings and application statuses can be tracked automatically rather than typed in by hand.
  • contacts.readonly — to read the author's own contacts, so that people already known to him can be matched to companies that are hiring.

Both permissions are read-only. WarmPath cannot send, modify, or delete email or contacts.

What it stores, and where

Extracted job postings, company names, application statuses, and contact details are stored in a PostgreSQL database on the author's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to a server, a hosted database, or any cloud service.

What it shares

Nothing. Data obtained through these permissions is never sold, transferred, or disclosed to any third party. It is not used for advertising, and it is not used to train any machine-learning model. There are no analytics and no third-party trackers in WarmPath.

Limited Use

WarmPath's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Data stays in the local database until the author deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions, which immediately ends WarmPath's ability to read anything.
